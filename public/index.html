How it works

Type a number, pick the unit you have and the unit you want. The answer updates as you type, and the table under the converter shows the same value in every other unit of that measure. Conversions use the exact definitions from the International System of Units and the 1959 international yard and pound agreement, and run entirely in your browser.

Each converter has its own page with a reference table and answers to common questions. You can bookmark or share any conversion: the address bar keeps the value and units you chose.

Questions

Are the results exact?

They are calculated from exact definitions and shown to 10 significant digits. Months and years in the time converter are calendar averages.

Do I need to install anything?

No. The converter works in any modern browser, on phones and desktops.

Can I share a conversion?

Yes. The page address updates with your value and units, so copy it from the address bar.
